Tick: Simple To-Do · Native for Mac

Support

Need help with Tick? Contact Yafei Wang at yfeiib@gmail.com. Include your macOS version, Tick version and a short description of the issue. Screenshots are helpful, but please remove private task details before sharing them.

Getting started

Add a task in the main window or press Command–Shift–N for Quick Add. Organize tasks with lists, tags, due dates and priorities. Click the Tick checkmark in the menu bar to open the focused task panel.

Change the language

Open Tick Settings → General → App Language. Tick follows your system language by default and supports English, Simplified Chinese, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Japanese and Korean. Your own task titles and notes are not automatically translated.

Reminders

If reminders are not appearing, check Tick’s notification permission in macOS System Settings → Notifications. Focus modes and system notification settings can affect when reminders are shown.

Your data

No account is required. Tasks are stored locally on your Mac. Settings → Data can export pending tasks as text. Keep a separate backup if you need to preserve your data before reinstalling or changing computers.

Weather and daily quotes

Weather and some daily quotes need an internet connection. If an online quote cannot be loaded, Tick can show a bundled localized quote. Core task management remains available offline.

Report a problem

Describe what you expected, what actually happened and the steps that reproduce the problem. Never include passwords or Apple account credentials. You may also email feature suggestions to the address above.
