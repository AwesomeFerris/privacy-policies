Support

Need help with Tick? Contact Yafei Wang at yfeiib@gmail.com. Include your macOS version, Tick version and a short description of the issue. Screenshots are helpful, but please remove private task details before sharing them.

Getting started

Add a task in the main window or press Command–Shift–N for Quick Add. Organize tasks with lists, tags, due dates and priorities. Click the Tick checkmark in the menu bar to open the focused task panel.

Change the language

Open Tick Settings → General → App Language. Tick follows your system language by default and supports English, Simplified Chinese, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Japanese and Korean. Your own task titles and notes are not automatically translated.

Reminders

If reminders are not appearing, check Tick’s notification permission in macOS System Settings → Notifications. Focus modes and system notification settings can affect when reminders are shown.

Your data

No Tick account is required. Tasks, notes, lists, preferences and widget snapshots are stored locally on your Mac; Tick does not upload task content to the developer or the weather and quote services. Settings → Data can export pending tasks as text. Keep a separate backup if you need to preserve your data before reinstalling or changing computers.

You can edit or delete tasks in Tick. Uninstalling the app alone may leave local sandbox files or backups. Contact us if you need help locating remaining Tick data. We cannot remotely access or erase your local database; exported files and backups remain under your control.

Weather and daily quotes

Weather uses wttr.in, Chinese online quotes use Hitokoto, and English online quotes use Quotable. These services receive your public IP address and ordinary request metadata. wttr.in estimates your approximate area from your IP address; Tick does not request GPS or macOS Location Services access. Requests do not include your task content or focus notes.

Requests can occur automatically when the relevant feature loads. Other supported interface languages use bundled translated quotes, and a bundled quote is available as a fallback if an online quote cannot be loaded. A fallback after a failed request does not mean no network request was attempted.

Privacy choices

Core task management remains available offline. The current version does not have a separate switch to disable every weather and quote request. To avoid these requests, use Tick without network access. Changing the quote language does not disable weather requests.

Third-party providers control their request logs, retention and processing locations. Removing local Tick data does not remove logs held by those providers. See the Privacy Policy for the full explanation. For information you sent directly to us in a support message, email yfeiib@gmail.com with your privacy request.
