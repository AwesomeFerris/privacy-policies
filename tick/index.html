Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: October 3, 2026

Your tasks stay on your Mac. Tick does not require an account and does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. Weather and some daily quotes use external online services, which receive ordinary network request information.

1. Who is responsible

Tick is developed by Yafei Wang. For questions about this policy or privacy requests, email yfeiib@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Tick stores your tasks, task notes and links, subtasks, lists, tags, priorities, dates, recurrence settings, completion history, daily focus notes and application preferences locally on your Mac. A local App Group container shares task data and snapshots with Tick’s widget and system integrations. These are local files, not a developer-operated cloud service.

Tick does not upload your task content or focus notes to the developer, weather service or quote services. The current version does not provide account registration or developer-operated cloud synchronization. macOS backups or other backup tools you enable may separately retain copies of local data.

3. Online weather and daily quotes

  • Weather: Tick requests weather from wttr.in. The provider uses the request’s IP address to estimate the area for weather. Tick does not request GPS or macOS Location Services access.
  • Chinese daily quotes: Tick requests quotes from Hitokoto at v1.hitokoto.cn.
  • English daily quotes: Tick requests quotes from Quotable at api.quotable.io.
  • Other supported languages and fallback: Tick can select a translated quote bundled with the app. Local selection does not send your task content to an AI service.

These requests use HTTPS and may occur automatically when the relevant feature loads. External providers receive your IP address and ordinary request metadata, such as the requested endpoint and request time. Their handling, logging, retention and processing locations are controlled by those providers; we do not control their server logs or promise that they retain no data. Requests do not contain your tasks, notes, email address or a Tick account identifier. Core task management works without an internet connection.

4. Notifications and Apple services

If you authorize notifications, Tick schedules local task reminders through macOS. Reminder text may be visible on your screen according to your system notification settings. You can change this permission in System Settings. Apple’s App Store, system diagnostics and other Apple services operate under Apple’s own privacy terms and your system choices, independently of Tick.

5. Support, analytics and sharing

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include, such as an issue description or diagnostic attachment. We use this information to respond and troubleshoot. Avoid sending sensitive task content unless necessary. We do not automatically receive your local task database or collect in-app analytics through a third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

We do not sell your personal information, use it for advertising profiles or track you across other apps or websites. Email and online service providers process information involved in the communications described above. We may disclose information we actually hold when legally required.

6. Retention and security

Local content remains on your Mac until you remove it. You can edit or delete tasks and export pending tasks as text from Settings → Data. App removal alone may leave sandbox files or backups; contact us if you need help locating and removing remaining Tick data. Copies you export or back up remain under your control.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle your request and any applicable legal obligations. Tick uses the macOS app sandbox and HTTPS for its online requests. Local data files are not separately encrypted by Tick; device encryption and access protection depend on your Mac’s settings. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

You can use core task features offline, control notification permission, edit or delete your local content and choose what to share in support requests. We cannot remotely access, export or erase a database that exists only on your Mac.

Where applicable law provides such rights, you may request access to, correction, deletion or a portable copy of personal information we hold, and may object to or request restriction of its processing. For EEA and UK users, this may also include the right to complain to your local data protection authority. For California users, we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

For support correspondence, we process the information to respond to your request and maintain the security of our communications, and where necessary to comply with legal obligations. Depending on your location, email and external online providers may process request information in other countries. Contact yfeiib@gmail.com to discuss information we hold and applicable rights.

8. Children

Tick is a general-purpose productivity app, not a service designed to collect children’s personal information. It has no accounts or social messaging features. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us in a support message, contact us so we can address it.

9. Policy updates

We may revise this policy when Tick’s features or data practices change. The updated policy will show a new date. Please review it when installing an update.
