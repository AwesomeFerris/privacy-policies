Tick: Simple To-Do · Yafei Wang

Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: October 3, 2026

Your tasks and notes stay on your Mac unless you choose to share them outside Tick. No Tick account is required, and the app does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Tick is not entirely offline: weather and some daily quotes make requests to external services. Those services receive your IP address and request metadata; the weather service also estimates your approximate area from your IP address.

1. Who is responsible

Tick: Simple To-Do is developed by Yafei Wang ("we" or "us"). This policy covers the macOS app, its bundled widget and privacy-related support correspondence. Yafei Wang is responsible for personal information received directly through support. For questions about this policy or privacy requests, email yfeiib@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Tick stores your tasks, task notes and links, subtasks, lists, tags, priorities, dates, recurrence settings, completion history, daily focus notes and application preferences locally on your Mac. A local App Group container shares task data and snapshots with Tick’s widget and system integrations. These are local files, not a developer-operated cloud service.

Tick does not upload your task content or focus notes to the developer, weather service or quote services. The current version does not provide account registration or developer-operated cloud synchronization. macOS backups or other backup tools you enable may separately retain copies of local data.

3. Online weather and daily quotes

  • Weather: Tick requests weather from wttr.in to display a weather icon. The service receives your IP address and uses it to estimate your approximate geographic area, such as a city or region. Tick does not request GPS or macOS Location Services access, and it does not send device-derived GPS coordinates. Weather requests can occur automatically when the menu bar panel loads. Successful requests are normally limited to one per hour while the app is running.
  • Chinese daily quotes: With Chinese selected, Tick requests a quote from Hitokoto at v1.hitokoto.cn. The request specifies literature and philosophy categories, not your task content.
  • English daily quotes: With English selected, Tick requests a quote from Quotable at api.quotable.io. The request specifies inspirational and motivational tags and a maximum quote length, not your task content.
  • Other supported languages and fallback: Tick can select a translated quote bundled with the app. Local selection does not send your task content to an AI service.

Online quote requests may occur automatically when a quote is needed, including after a language change, or when you use the quote refresh action. The language-specific endpoint reveals which quote source is requested. An offline fallback can be shown after an unsuccessful online request; this does not mean no request was attempted.

All three services receive the originating public IP address and ordinary HTTPS request metadata, including the endpoint, query parameters, request time and standard headers sent by the networking system. These requests do not include your tasks, notes, email address, advertising identifier or a Tick account identifier. Tick does not operate a server that receives these weather or quote requests.

Third-party processing and logs: Providers can process request information to deliver content, operate and secure their services, and maintain server logs. The wttr.in project documentation describes IP-geolocation caching and access logs used for service statistics. We therefore do not describe weather requests as anonymous or promise that request information is discarded immediately after a response. We do not have a verified retention period for the public weather or quote services, control their server logs, or guarantee their processing locations or de-identification practices. Their own applicable terms and privacy notices govern their handling of request information.

Your choice: Core task management works without an internet connection. The current version does not provide a separate in-app switch to disable every weather and online quote request. To avoid these requests, use Tick without network access. Selecting a language that uses bundled quotes avoids that language's online quote request, but does not disable weather requests.

4. Notifications and Apple services

If you authorize notifications, Tick schedules local task reminders through macOS. Reminder text may be visible on your screen according to your system notification settings. You can change this permission in System Settings. Apple’s App Store, system diagnostics and other Apple services operate under Apple’s own privacy terms and your system choices, independently of Tick.

5. Support, analytics and sharing

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include, such as an issue description or diagnostic attachment. We use this information to respond and troubleshoot. Avoid sending sensitive task content unless necessary. We do not automatically receive your local task database or collect in-app analytics through a third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

We, as the developer, do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, build advertising profiles or track you across other apps or websites. Tick does not include advertising or tracking SDKs. These statements describe our practices and are not a promise that external providers keep no request logs. Email and online service providers process information involved in the communications described above. We may disclose information we actually hold when legally required.

6. Retention and security

Local content remains on your Mac until you remove it. You can edit or delete tasks and export pending tasks as text from Settings → Data. App removal alone may leave sandbox files or backups; contact us if you need help locating and removing remaining Tick data. Copies you export or back up remain under your control.

Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle your request and any applicable legal obligations; you may contact us to request deletion of information we hold. Third-party request logs and caches are separate from your local Tick data. Deleting a task, uninstalling Tick or asking us to delete support correspondence does not delete information retained by a weather, quote or email provider. We cannot promise a deletion deadline or retention period on another provider's behalf.

Tick uses the macOS app sandbox and HTTPS for its online requests. Local data files are not separately encrypted by Tick; device encryption and access protection depend on your Mac’s settings. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

You can use core task features offline, control notification permission, edit or delete your local content and choose what to share in support requests. We cannot remotely access, export or erase a database that exists only on your Mac.

Where applicable law provides such rights, you may request access to, correction, deletion or a portable copy of personal information we hold, and may object to or request restriction of its processing. For EEA and UK users, this may also include the right to complain to your local data protection authority. For California users, we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

For support correspondence, we process the information to respond to your request and maintain the security of our communications, and where necessary to comply with legal obligations. Depending on your location, email and external online providers may process request information in other countries. Contact yfeiib@gmail.com to discuss information we hold and applicable rights.

8. Children

Tick is a general-purpose productivity app, not a service designed to collect children’s personal information. It has no accounts or social messaging features. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us in a support message, contact us so we can address it.

9. Policy updates

We may revise this policy when Tick’s features or data practices change. The updated policy will show a new date and will be available at our privacy policy page. Please review the published policy when installing an update. A copy bundled with an older app build may not include later clarifications.
