GoSwim Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 1, 2026

GoSwim (the “App”) is an offline-first swim training analysis app. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Our Core Promise

The developer does not collect personal data through developer-operated servers, sell personal data, or use it for advertising. Specifically, the App:

  • contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs;
  • sends no workout data to an external server unless you enable and configure the optional open-source AI service;
  • does not track your behavior or use tracking technologies; and
  • has no account system and requires no registration or sign-in.

Health Data (HealthKit)

With your permission, the App reads swimming workouts, workout distance, stroke data, heart rate, active energy, perceived effort, heart-rate variability, date of birth, biological sex, and body mass from Apple HealthKit. This information is used to provide workout summaries, training-load and recovery insights, personal records, and training suggestions. This version does not create or write new HealthKit records.

Health data is processed on your device by default and is not used for advertising, marketing, or data brokerage. A workout summary is sent externally only when you configure the optional AI service described below. You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in Settings or the Health app.

Optional AI Commentary

The App offers optional AI-generated workout commentary. Apple’s on-device model processes data on your device. The open-source model connection is disabled by default. If you enable it, you must provide a service address under your control. A workout summary may then be sent to that address and may include stroke type, distance, duration, pace, SWOLF, stroke efficiency, average heart rate, and analysis points.

This transfer is initiated and configured by you. The developer cannot access data sent to your configured service and is not responsible for that service’s data-processing practices.

On-Device Storage and Sync

Workout caches, workout notes, goals, profile information, and preferences are stored locally on your device. This release does not enable GoSwim iCloud synchronization. Any HealthKit synchronization is controlled by your Apple account and system settings; the developer cannot access your iCloud data.

Deleting the App removes the App’s locally stored data.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, this page and its effective date will be updated.
