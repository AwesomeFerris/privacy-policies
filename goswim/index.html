GoSwim 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 5 日

GoSwim（以下简称"本应用"）是一款离线优先的游泳训练分析应用。我们非常重视你的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何处理数据。

核心承诺

本应用不收集、不上传、不出售你的任何个人数据。具体而言：

  • 不包含任何第三方分析、广告、追踪或崩溃上报 SDK；
  • 不向你或任何第三方的服务器发送数据；
  • 不进行任何形式的用户行为追踪，不使用任何追踪技术；
  • 没有账号系统，不需要注册或登录。

健康数据（HealthKit）

本应用在你授权后通过 Apple HealthKit 读取你的游泳训练、心率、心率变异性（HRV）等数据，用于在你的设备本地计算训练负荷、恢复状态和训练建议。你也可以授权本应用将训练记录写回健康 App。

所有健康数据仅在设备本地处理，永远不会离开你的设备，也不会用于广告或与任何第三方共享。你可以随时在系统设置中撤销授权，撤销后相关功能将不可用。

本地 AI 点评功能（可选，默认关闭）

本应用提供一个可选的 AI 训练点评功能，默认关闭。如果你主动开启，需要自行填写由你控制的服务地址（例如你电脑上运行的 Ollama 服务）。开启后，本次训练的摘要数据会被发送到你自己填写的地址，用于生成点评文字。该数据发送完全由你掌控，开发者无法获取这些数据，也不承担你自行配置的服务所产生数据处理责任。

iCloud 同步

你的偏好设置（如泳池长度、单位制、周目标等）可通过苹果的 iCloud 键值存储在你的设备间同步。该同步由苹果在你自己的 iCloud 账号下完成，开发者无法访问你的 iCloud 数据。你可以在系统设置中关闭 iCloud 同步。

本地存储

训练设置与缓存数据保存在你的设备本地。卸载应用即删除全部本地数据。

儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不 knowingly 收集任何儿童的个人信息。

政策变更

如本政策有实质性变更，我们会更新此页面并修改生效日期。继续使用本应用即表示接受更新后的政策。

联系方式

如有任何问题，请通过 GitHub 提交 issue：AwesomeFerris/privacy-policies

GoSwim Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

GoSwim ("the App") is an offline-first swim training analysis app. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the App handles data.

Our Core Promise

The App does not collect, upload, or sell any of your personal data. Specifically, the App:

  • contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs;
  • sends no data to our servers or any third-party server;
  • does not track your behavior or use any tracking technology;
  • has no account system and requires no registration or sign-in.

Health Data (HealthKit)

With your permission, the App reads your swim workouts, heart rate, and heart-rate variability (HRV) from Apple HealthKit to compute training load, recovery, and training suggestions — all locally on your device. With your permission it may also save workout records back to the Health app.

All health data is processed on-device only. It never leaves your device, is never used for advertising, and is never shared with any third party. You may revoke access at any time in system Settings; related features will then become unavailable.

Optional Local AI Commentary (off by default)

The App offers an optional AI workout commentary feature. It is disabled by default. If you choose to enable it, you must provide a server address under your own control (for example, an Ollama service running on your computer). When enabled, a summary of the current workout is sent to the address you configured to generate commentary text.

This transfer is entirely under your control. The developer has no access to this data and is not responsible for data processing performed by services you configure yourself.

iCloud Sync

Your preferences (pool length, unit system, weekly goals, etc.) can sync across your devices via Apple's iCloud Key-Value Store. Syncing is performed by Apple under your own iCloud account; the developer cannot access your iCloud data. You can disable iCloud sync in system Settings.

Local Storage

Settings and cached data are stored locally on your device. Deleting the App removes all local data.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and revise the effective date. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
