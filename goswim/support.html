GoSwim Support

Need help or found a problem?

Open a request in GitHub Issues. Include the App version, iOS version, reproduction steps, and screenshots that do not expose sensitive health data.

Swimming Workouts Do Not Appear

  1. Open the Health app and confirm that swimming workouts are present.
  2. Go to Health → profile picture → Privacy → Apps and Services → GoSwim.
  3. Allow the relevant data access, then reopen GoSwim.

AI Commentary Cannot Connect

Confirm that your phone can reach the model service you configured and that GoSwim has local-network permission when required. This feature is off by default and does not connect to a developer-operated server.

Privacy

Read the GoSwim Privacy Policy. Do not publicly include your date of birth, detailed heart-rate data, or complete workout records in a support request.
