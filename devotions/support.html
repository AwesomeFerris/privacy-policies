Frequently asked questions

A place to begin.

What devices does Still support?

Still is made for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No account or subscription is required to access the 24 guided practices, reflection prompts, personal journal, prayer plans, and 40-day journey in this version.

Can I listen without an internet connection?

A morning prayer in each supported prayer language is included with the app. Other recordings download when you request them. After a recording finishes downloading, you can listen to it offline on the same device. Reading and personal reflections are also available offline.

What if an audio download does not finish?

Check your internet connection and available device storage, then use the retry option in the player. You can read the prayer while the recording is unavailable. If the issue continues, send support the prayer title, prayer language, and any error message shown.

Can I choose a different language for prayers?

Open You, then Reminders & appearance, and choose Language. You can select the interface language and prayer language separately. Prayer text and audio are available in English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, and Brazilian Portuguese. The interface supports 16 languages.

Who reads the guided prayers?

The audio uses synthesized voices. You can adjust playback speed in the player, pause whenever you need, or switch to Read to follow the full prayer text.

Where are my journal and prayer list stored?

Your reflections, prayer list, saved practices, and progress are stored on your device. Still does not provide an account or cloud synchronization. You can edit, delete, or export your journal entries from the app.

How do I set a daily reminder?

Open You, then Reminders & appearance, to choose your reminder preferences. If reminders are enabled but do not arrive, check that notifications are allowed for Still in your device settings.

What happens if I miss a day in a journey or plan?

Your completed progress is preserved. Return to the 40-day journey or your seven-day prayer plan when you are ready.
