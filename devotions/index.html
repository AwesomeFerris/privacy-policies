Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026 · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Still: Daily Devotions keeps your journal, prayer list, and practice progress on your device. The app does not require an account or upload these personal records to us. Audio downloads, optional support emails, and visits to this website have separate data practices described below.

This policy explains how Yafei Wang ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Still: Daily Devotions ("Still" or "the app") and its support and legal webpages. For privacy questions, contact yfeiib@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

Still stores information locally to provide its features, including:

  • Journal entries and any edits you make.
  • Your prayer list, notes, prayer activity, and answered-prayer status.
  • Saved practices, listening time, playback positions, and completion history.
  • Journey and prayer-plan progress, quiet-timer activity, and weekly goals.
  • Language, appearance, playback, and reminder preferences.

These records are processed on your device. We do not receive a copy of them, operate an account system, or provide cloud synchronization for them. In-app recommendations and progress displays use local records. The app does not include advertising, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Your reflections may contain personal or sensitive information, including religious beliefs or details about other people. You choose what to write. That content is not uploaded to our servers through normal use of the app.

2. Audio downloads and narration

A morning prayer in each supported prayer language is included with the app. Other recordings are delivered through Apple's hosted asset-download service when requested. Downloaded audio is validated and stored on your device for later offline listening; those downloaded files are excluded from device backups.

Apple handles the delivery requests under its own Privacy Policy. Still does not send journal entries, personal prayer-list notes, or your practice history with those requests.

The guided recordings use synthesized narration. Your journal entries and personal reflections are not submitted to an online translation, voice-generation, or AI service. Prayer text and translations are included with the app.

3. Reminders and permissions

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Still asks for notification permission when you enable reminders. You can turn reminders off in the app or manage notification permission in your device settings.

The app does not request access to your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or health records to provide its prayer and reflection features.

4. Sharing and external services

When you use a sharing or journal-export feature, Still opens the system share sheet. You choose what to share and the recipient or destination app. The recipient's service may receive and retain what you send under its own privacy practices. Exported files are separate from the original records in Still.

Opening external links takes you to another service. Apple's App Store, system services, device backups, and any diagnostic sharing you enable through Apple are governed by Apple's settings and policies. Still does not add a separate analytics or advertising service.

5. Support messages

If you email yfeiib@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to include. This may include your device model, operating-system version, app version, or an error screenshot.

We use that information to respond to you, investigate the issue, and handle related follow-up or privacy requests. You can describe an issue without sending private journal entries or prayer-list details. Support correspondence is processed through email providers and may be handled where those providers operate.

We do not sell personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or use support messages to build advertising profiles. We may disclose support information when required by applicable law or necessary to address a legal claim.

6. Website visits

These webpages are intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting a hosted page sends ordinary web-request information, such as your IP address and browser request details, to the hosting service. The host's handling of that information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We have not added analytics scripts, advertising pixels, cookies, embedded third-party media, or contact forms to these pages. A support email link opens your email application; a message is sent only if you choose to send it.

7. Retention, deletion, and backups

Local records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. You can edit or delete journal entries and prayer-list items in Still, and export journal content using the app's export feature. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local app data. Exports and previously created device backups are managed separately.

Operating-system or device backup settings may include local personal records. Still does not provide its own backup or cross-device sync service. Downloaded audio is excluded from backups and can require downloading again after reinstalling or restoring the app.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request, related follow-up, and applicable legal obligations. We do not have access to your local journal or practice records and cannot retrieve or remotely delete them. Hosting and email providers apply their own retention policies.

8. Security

Still uses the operating system's app-storage controls, and its personal practice-record file is saved with iOS file protection. Your device security, passcode, backup choices, and the services you select for sharing also affect how your information is protected. No storage or communication method can provide an absolute security guarantee.

9. Your choices and rights

You control local entries, exports, sharing, and reminder settings through the app and your device. For personal information you have provided to us in a support request, you may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion, or to raise a privacy concern.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to portability, restriction of processing, objection, withdrawal of consent where consent is used, and a complaint to a relevant privacy authority. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law and may need information sufficient to verify the request. Exercising a privacy right does not change your access to the app's local features.

We use information you voluntarily provide to handle the support you request, maintain appropriate correspondence records, and meet legal obligations where applicable. We do not treat reading this policy as consent to advertising or unrelated processing.

10. Children's privacy

Still does not create user accounts or child profiles, and its local-storage design applies to all users. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through support correspondence without any authorization required by applicable law. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent us personal information that should be removed, contact us so we can review and address the request.

11. Policy changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The revised policy will show a new last-updated date. If a change requires notice or consent under applicable law, we will provide the notice or obtain the consent required before applying it.
