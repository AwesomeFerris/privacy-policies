1. Scope and contact

This policy applies to Slumber, a sleep tracking app. The Slumber app provider, as identified on its App Store listing (“we”, “us”), is responsible for the processing described here. Contact us about privacy, support, or data rights at yfeiib@gmail.com.

This policy distinguishes information processed on your device from information that leaves your device when you choose to send feedback or share data. Using the app does not, by itself, authorize additional data sharing.

2. Information processed on your device

  • Sounds and recordings: After you grant microphone access and start a session, the app analyzes sleep sounds, stores recordings or sound clips, and creates events such as snoring, coughing, and environmental noise.
  • Sleep records: Session times, estimated sleep stages, scores, trends, sound events, waveforms, breathing-related indicators, and any notes, tags, lifestyle factors, and wake-up mood you enter.
  • Motion: Device acceleration during recording supports analysis; related summaries may be stored.
  • Apple Health: Within the permissions you grant, the app can access heart rate and sleep data and write app-generated sleep records to Apple Health. Availability depends on your device and permissions.
  • Settings: Language, alarms, sleep goals, reminders, and other preferences are stored locally.

These data support the recording, analysis, playback, statistics, and reminder features you request. The current version requires no account, performs its core analysis on-device, does not automatically upload sleep records or audio to our servers, and has no app-operated cloud sync.

3. Feedback and exports you choose to send

In-app feedback is not anonymous. Submitting feedback prepares an email draft with a ZIP attachment. The current attachment includes all saved sleep sessions on your device, such as times, sleep and heart-rate metrics, sound and breathing events, notes, tags, mood, and recording filenames. It also includes diagnostics: app version, build number, device model and model identifier, operating system version, locale, and time zone. The model identifier describes the hardware model; it is not a device serial number or advertising identifier.

The current feedback ZIP contains structured records, not the actual audio files. If you separately attach or share a recording, that selected audio will also be sent to your chosen recipient.

When you confirm sending in the system mail interface, your message, attachment, and sender email address are sent to yfeiib@gmail.com. You can cancel or send a separate plain email without sleep attachments. Feedback is optional and is not required for core sleep features. Review attachments and avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

We use received information only to respond, investigate issues, and fix related features, not for advertising, health-data trading, or training general-purpose AI models. Exports and system sharing send data to the apps or recipients you select; their subsequent handling is governed by their own privacy practices.

4. Permissions and your choices

Microphone access supports sound recording and analysis; motion access supports sleep analysis; Apple Health access supports reading authorized data and writing sleep records; notifications support reminders and alarm-related notices. Live Activities may show recording status or sleep-related information on the lock screen and can be managed in system settings.

You can stop recording and manage or revoke permissions in iOS settings and Apple Health. Revocation stops subsequent access requiring that permission and may limit related features, but does not automatically delete existing local records or emails already sent. Use the record deletion controls or contact us about feedback we have received.

5. Third parties, sharing, and international processing

The current app has no third-party advertising, cross-app tracking, or analytics service that automatically uploads usage activity. Its on-device models and inference components perform local computation; using them does not automatically send audio to model providers. We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use health data for marketing or advertising.

When you choose to send feedback, your email provider and our Gmail provider process the email. Messages may be processed or stored outside your country or region. Google processing is governed by its Privacy Policy. Apple Health, the App Store, system backups, and diagnostics are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy and your system settings. System backups, exported copies you save, and third-party shared copies are not directly managed by the app.

We disclose received information only as necessary to handle your support request, at your express direction, or as required by law. Legally required data protection measures apply to international processing for which we are responsible.

These policy and terms pages themselves contain no analytics scripts, advertising, or cookies. Your hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser information, and access logs when you visit the hosted pages, depending on its settings and policies.

6. Retention, deletion, and security

Local records remain until you delete the relevant records or delete the app; offloading an app while keeping its data is different from deleting it. Deleting a sleep record also attempts to remove its associated recording, sound clips, and supporting files. Records written to Apple Health must be managed separately in Apple Health. Local deletion does not automatically delete Health records, backups, exports, or recipients’ copies.

Support emails and attachments are kept only as long as needed to handle the request, resolve related technical issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. When those purposes end, they are deleted or anonymized; unnecessary health attachments should be deleted promptly. You may request earlier deletion, except where retention is legally required.

Local data is protected by the iOS app sandbox and device security mechanisms. Use a device passcode and share exports carefully. The app does not guarantee end-to-end encryption for email or third-party sharing. Access to received support information is limited to people who need it to handle your request. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your privacy rights

You can view, export, and delete sleep records on your device and manage permissions. For feedback information we receive, contact yfeiib@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion, a copy, restriction, or withdrawal of consent. Describe your request and the relevant email; we ask only for information necessary to verify it, not a new complete set of sleep records.

Where applicable law provides, you may also have rights to portability, objection, complaints to a data protection authority, and other regional rights. We rely on your consent for processing that requires it. Contact information needed for ordinary support may be processed as necessary to respond to your request or under legitimate interests permitted by applicable law. Any additional processing of health information is limited to the support purpose you expressly authorize and is subject to sensitive-data protections.

Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. We respond within applicable legal deadlines and do not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights. Declining to send feedback does not affect core on-device features.

8. Minors, policy updates, and contact

Minors should use the app with appropriate guardian guidance and should not send health records or other personal information without any legally required guardian consent. If you believe a minor has sent information we should not process, contact yfeiib@gmail.com; we will investigate and delete or restrict it as required by applicable law.

We will update this policy and its revision date when features or data practices change. Changes requiring additional consent will require that consent separately; updating this policy does not automatically expand an existing authorization.
