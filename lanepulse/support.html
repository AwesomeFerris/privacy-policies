Frequently asked questions

Can I use LanePulse without Apple Health access?

Yes. Foundation lessons, technique practice and manually entered practice records can be used without Health access. Imported swimming records and health-based analysis require permission and relevant data in Apple Health.

Why are my swimming workouts missing?

First check that the swimming workouts appear in Apple Health on this iPhone. In the Health app or the iPhone health privacy settings, review the data LanePulse is allowed to read. Allow the workout and metric types you want to use, then reopen LanePulse. Check the selected month in History; the Progress page shows recent training, so older swims may appear only in History. Health authorization does not create missing workout data.

Does LanePulse record a swim on Apple Watch?

This version reads swimming workouts already saved in Apple Health, including compatible workouts recorded by Apple Watch. It does not include an Apple Watch recording app, and does not write, modify or delete workouts in Apple Health.

How do I start as a beginner?

Open Learn and choose a starting point that matches your experience. Foundation lessons focus on getting comfortable in the water, breathing, balance and short swims. You can record how practice felt without a distance or pace target. Begin water exercises with a qualified instructor present.

What are Practice and the practice journal for?

Practice provides learning tasks or training sessions appropriate to your starting point. Use Log a practice to record your experience and, if you want, distance and time. You can review and edit your practice journal. Any suggested adjustment to the next session is applied only when you choose to accept it.

Why is my recent distance zero when I have older swims?

Progress summarizes the last seven days. If no swimming workouts are available during that period, the recent distance can be zero while History still contains earlier workouts. Manually entered lesson practice and imported swimming workouts are displayed separately.

How do I change the language or training preferences?

Open Profile, then Settings to change the app language and personal preferences. Training preferences include your training days and reminder settings.

Why am I not receiving training reminders?

Review the reminder settings and selected training days, and check that notifications are allowed for LanePulse in iPhone Settings. Focus settings or scheduled notification delivery can affect when a notification is presented. Reminders are local notifications.

How do I remove my data?

To remove local LanePulse data, open iPhone Settings, then General, iPhone Storage and LanePulse, and choose Delete App. Offload App keeps app data. Deleting LanePulse does not delete original Apple Health records. Revoking Health access stops permitted access but does not erase previously saved local records. Backup copies are managed by your system backup settings.

Does the app require an account, an AI server or an IP address?

No. This version requires no account or AI server configuration. Analysis uses workout metrics and fixed rules on your device. Health data and practice records are not uploaded to the developer or external AI services.
